# Team Defaulters - Pricing Strategy 2026

At Team Defaulters, we believe in **transparent, predictable pricing** with no hidden fees. Our pricing model is designed to scale with your business, offering flexibility for startups and enterprise-grade solutions for large organizations.

## Reserved Instances & Committed Use

### Reserved Instance Pricing

Commit to specific instance types for 1 or 3 years and save significantly.

**1-Year Commitment:**
- **All Upfront:** 35% discount
- **Partial Upfront (50% down):** 30% discount
- **Monthly Payments:** 25% discount

**3-Year Commitment:**
- **All Upfront:** 65% discount
- **Partial Upfront (50% down):** 60% discount
- **Monthly Payments:** 55% discount

**Example Calculation:**

N1.large normally costs $232/month ($2,784/year).

**1-Year Reserved (All Upfront):**
- Cost: $1,810 (35% savings = $974 saved)

**3-Year Reserved (All Upfront):**
- Cost: $2,920 for 3 years ($81/month equivalent)
- Savings: $5,442 over 3 years

### Committed Use Discounts (Flexible)

Don't want to commit to specific instance types? Use Committed Use Discounts.

**How It Works:**
- Commit to a minimum monthly spend (e.g., $5,000/month)
- Use any combination of services (compute, storage, etc.)
- Receive a percentage discount on all usage

**Discount Tiers:**

| Monthly Commitment | Discount |
|--------------------|----------|
| $5,000/month | 15% |
| $10,000/month | 20% |
| $25,000/month | 25% |
| $50,000/month | 30% |
| $100,000/month | 35% |

**Contract Terms:** 1 or 3 years

## Pricing Transparency

### No Hidden Fees

We do NOT charge for:
- API requests (except Vortex storage API requests)
- Inbound data transfer
- Data transfer between services in the same region
- Account setup or cancellation
- Support tickets (within your tier)

### What You DO Pay For

- Compute time (per second billing, 1-minute minimum)
- Storage (per GB/month)
- Outbound data transfer
- Load balancers (per hour + data processed)
- Public IP addresses ($3/month each)
