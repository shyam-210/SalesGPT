# Team Defaulters - Startup Program Eligibility

## Program Overview

The **Team Defaulters Startup Program** is designed to help early-stage companies build and scale on world-class cloud infrastructure without breaking the bank. We believe in empowering the next generation of innovators.

## Eligibility Criteria

### 🔒 CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

**Startups are eligible for $5,000 in cloud credits ONLY if they meet ALL of the following criteria:**

1. **Funding Stage:** Must have raised **Series A funding or later**
2. **Company Age:** Less than 5 years old (from date of incorporation)
3. **First-Time Customer:** Not currently a Team Defaulters customer
4. **Active Development:** Product must be in active development or launched
5. **No Prior Credits:** Has not previously received Team Defaulters startup credits

### Why Series A?

We focus on Series A+ companies because:
- They have validated product-market fit
- They have committed investors backing them
- They are ready to scale (our infrastructure's sweet spot)
- They can provide meaningful case studies and testimonials

**Pre-Series A Startups:**  
If you have not yet raised Series A, you are **NOT eligible** for the $5,000 credit program. However, you can:
- Use our **Free Tier** (750 hours/month of N1.small + 5 GB storage)
- Apply for our **Accelerator Partner Program** (if you're part of Y Combinator, Techstars, etc.)

## Credit Details

### What You Get

**$5,000 in Cloud Credits:**
- Valid for **12 months** from activation
- Can be used for ANY Team Defaulters service (Nebula Compute, Vortex Storage, etc.)
- No restrictions on instance types (yes, you can use GPUs!)
- Credits applied automatically to your monthly bill

**Additional Benefits:**
- **Free Professional Tier Support** for 12 months ($499/month value)
  - 24-hour response time
  - Chat + email support
  - Monthly architecture review call
- **Startup Community Access:**
  - Private Slack channel with other startups
  - Monthly webinars on scaling best practices
  - Networking events (virtual and in-person)
- **Co-Marketing Opportunities:**
  - Feature in Team Defaulters case studies
  - Logo on our startup partners page
  - Social media shoutouts

### Credit Usage Rules

**What Credits Cover:**
- ✅ Compute instances (all types, including GPUs)
- ✅ Storage (Vortex Standard, IA, Glacier)
- ✅ Data transfer
- ✅ Load balancers
- ✅ Snapshots and backups

**What Credits DON'T Cover:**
- ❌ Overages after credits are exhausted
- ❌ Third-party marketplace software
- ❌ Domain registration fees
- ❌ SSL certificate purchases (free Let's Encrypt certs are available)

**Credit Expiration:**
- Unused credits expire after 12 months (no extensions)
- Credits are non-transferable
- Credits cannot be redeemed for cash

## Application Process

### Step 1: Submit Application

**Apply at:** startups.teamdefaulters.com/apply

**Required Information:**
- Company name and website
- Founder names and LinkedIn profiles
- Brief product description (2-3 sentences)
- **Funding details:**
  - Total funding raised
  - Series A lead investor name
  - Date of Series A close
  - Link to funding announcement (TechCrunch, press release, etc.)
- Expected monthly cloud spend
- Current cloud provider (if any)

### Step 2: Verification

Our team will verify your eligibility:
- **Funding Verification:** We check Crunchbase, PitchBook, or request a cap table excerpt
- **Company Verification:** Confirm incorporation date via public records
- **Investor Verification:** May contact your lead investor for confirmation

**Timeline:** 3-5 business days

### Step 3: Approval & Onboarding

Once approved:
1. **Welcome Email:** Includes credit code and onboarding instructions
2. **Kickoff Call (Optional):** 30-minute call with Solutions Architect to discuss your architecture
3. **Credit Activation:** Credits activate immediately upon account creation

**Rejection:**
- If rejected, you'll receive an email with the reason
- You can reapply once you meet the criteria (e.g., after raising Series A)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply if I raised a large seed round instead of Series A?

**No.** Our program specifically requires Series A funding. However, if your seed round was $5M+ and led by a top-tier VC, email startups@teamdefaulters.com for a case-by-case review.

### What if I'm bootstrapped and profitable?

Unfortunately, bootstrapped companies are not eligible for the $5,000 credit program. However:
- Use our Free Tier
- Consider our Pay-As-You-Go plan (no commitment)
- If you're doing $1M+ ARR, contact sales for volume discounts

### Can I stack credits with other promotions?

**No.** Startup credits cannot be combined with:
- Trial credits ($300 for new customers)
- Referral credits
- Conference/event promotions

### What happens if I run out of credits mid-month?

Your services will continue running, and you'll be billed for overages at standard pay-as-you-go rates. We recommend:
- Setting up billing alerts at 50%, 75%, 90% credit usage
- Monitoring usage in the console: console.teamdefaulters.com/billing

### Can I get more credits if I run out early?

**Generally, no.** The $5,000 credit is a one-time grant. However:
- If you're growing rapidly (>100% MoM), contact your account manager
- We may offer additional credits on a case-by-case basis for high-growth startups

### Do I need a credit card to apply?

**Yes.** You must provide a credit card for:
- Identity verification
- Billing for usage beyond credits
- Preventing abuse

Your card will NOT be charged until credits are exhausted.
