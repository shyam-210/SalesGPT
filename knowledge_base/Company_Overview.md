# Team Defaulters - Company Overview

## Mission Statement

At **Team Defaulters**, we believe that infrastructure should be invisible. Our mission is to provide enterprise-grade cloud solutions that deliver **Zero Downtime** and infinite scalability, allowing businesses to focus on innovation rather than operations.

## Vision

To become the world's most trusted cloud infrastructure provider by 2030, powering the next generation of digital transformation across industries.

## Core Values

### 1. Reliability First
We guarantee 99.99% uptime across all our services. Our infrastructure is built on redundant systems across multiple availability zones.

### 2. Customer Obsession
Every decision we make starts with the customer. Our 24/7 support team ensures you're never alone in your cloud journey.

### 3. Innovation at Scale
We invest 25% of our annual revenue into R&D, constantly pushing the boundaries of what's possible in cloud computing.

## Company History

Founded in 2018 by a team of ex-AWS and Google Cloud engineers, Team Defaulters emerged from a simple observation: existing cloud providers were too complex and too expensive for mid-market companies.

**Key Milestones:**
- **2018:** Company founded in San Francisco
- **2019:** Launched Nebula Compute platform
- **2020:** Achieved SOC2 Type II certification
- **2021:** Expanded to Europe (Frankfurt, London data centers)
- **2022:** Introduced Vortex Storage with 11 nines of durability
- **2023:** Reached $100M ARR milestone
- **2024:** Launched AI/ML GPU clusters
- **2025:** Opened APAC headquarters in Singapore
- **2026:** Now serving 5,000+ enterprise customers globally
