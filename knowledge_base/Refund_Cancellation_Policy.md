# Team Defaulters - Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Overview

This document outlines Team Defaulters' policies regarding refunds, cancellations, and account termination. Please read carefully before subscribing to our services.

## Refund Policy

### 🔒 CRITICAL POLICY: 30-Day Refund Window

**Team Defaulters offers refunds ONLY within 30 days of the initial charge.**

**Eligibility:**
- Refund requests must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the charge date
- Applies to subscription fees (Starter, Professional, Enterprise tiers)
- Does NOT apply to pay-as-you-go usage charges

**After 30 Days:**
- **No refunds will be issued** for any reason
- This includes service dissatisfaction, unused credits, or account closure
- All sales are final after the 30-day window

### What Is Refundable

**✅ Refundable (Within 30 Days):**
- Monthly subscription fees (Starter, Professional tiers)
- Annual subscription fees (prorated for unused months)
- Reserved instance upfront payments (if not yet activated)
- Overpayments or billing errors

**❌ NOT Refundable:**
- Pay-as-you-go usage charges (compute, storage, data transfer)
- Consumed credits (trial credits, startup credits)
- Third-party marketplace purchases
- Domain registration fees
- SSL certificate purchases
- Professional services fees
- Support add-ons (if support was provided)

### How to Request a Refund

**Step 1: Submit Request**
- Email: billing@teamdefaulters.com
- Subject: "Refund Request - [Account ID]"
- Include:
  - Account ID
  - Charge date and amount
  - Reason for refund request
  - Invoice number

**Step 2: Review Process**
- Team Defaulters will review within **5 business days**
- We may request additional information or clarification

**Step 3: Refund Issued**
- If approved, refund processed within **10 business days**
- Refund issued to original payment method
- Credit card refunds may take 5-10 additional days to appear (depends on your bank)

### Refund Exceptions

**Prorated Refunds (Annual Subscriptions):**
- If you cancel an annual subscription within 30 days, you receive a **prorated refund** for unused months
- **Example:** You paid $4,790 for Professional annual plan. You cancel after 2 months. Refund: $4,790 × (10/12) = $3,992

**No Refunds for Abuse:**
- If your account is terminated for violating our Acceptable Use Policy, **no refund** will be issued
- This includes cryptocurrency mining, DDoS attacks, illegal content, etc.

**Enterprise Contracts:**
- Refund terms are governed by your Master Service Agreement (MSA)
- Contact your Technical Account Manager (TAM) for refund requests

## Cancellation Policy

### 🔒 CRITICAL POLICY: 15-Day Cancellation Notice Required

**To cancel your Team Defaulters subscription or services, you must provide a minimum of 15 calendar days' advance notice.**

**How This Works:**
- Submit cancellation request at least **15 days before** your next billing date
- If you cancel with less than 15 days' notice, you will be charged for the next billing cycle
- **Example:** Your billing date is the 1st of each month. To avoid being charged for March, you must cancel by February 14th.

### How to Cancel

**Method 1: Self-Service (Recommended)**
1. Log in to console: console.teamdefaulters.com
2. Navigate to **Billing > Subscriptions**
3. Click **Cancel Subscription**
4. Select cancellation date (must be 15+ days in future)
5. Confirm cancellation

**Method 2: Email**
1. Email: billing@teamdefaulters.com
2. Subject: "Cancellation Request - [Account ID]"
3. Include:
   - Account ID
   - Desired cancellation date (15+ days from today)
   - Reason for cancellation (optional but helpful)

**Method 3: Phone (Enterprise Only)**
1. Call: +1 (888) 333-CLOUD
2. Request cancellation with support agent
3. Confirmation email sent within 24 hours

### What Happens After Cancellation

**Immediate Effects:**
- You retain access to services until the end of your current billing period
- No new charges will be applied after cancellation date
- Unused credits expire (no refunds for unused credits)

**30 Days After Cancellation:**
- All data is **soft-deleted** (recoverable if you change your mind)
- Instances are stopped (not deleted)
- Storage is retained (but you're charged for storage until you delete it)

**90 Days After Cancellation:**
- All data is **permanently deleted** (unrecoverable)
- Instances, snapshots, and backups are destroyed
- Audit logs are retained for 1 year (compliance requirement)

**Important:**
- **You are responsible for deleting your data** if you want to avoid storage charges after cancellation
- **We do NOT automatically delete your data** to prevent accidental data loss
- To delete all data immediately, use the "Delete All Resources" option in the console

### Cancellation Fees

**No Cancellation Fees:**
- Starter and Professional tiers: **No cancellation fee**
- Pay-as-you-go: **No cancellation fee** (cancel anytime)

**Early Termination Fees (Reserved Instances & Enterprise):**
- **Reserved Instances (1-year):** 25% of remaining contract value
- **Reserved Instances (3-year):** 50% of remaining contract value
- **Enterprise Contracts:** Per Master Service Agreement (typically 30-50% of remaining value)

**Example:**
- You purchased a 3-year reserved instance for $10,000 (paid upfront)
- You cancel after 1 year
- Early termination fee: $10,000 × (2/3) × 50% = $3,333
- No refund issued (you forfeit remaining value)

### Pausing Your Account (Alternative to Cancellation)

**Don't want to cancel permanently?** Consider pausing your account.

**How It Works:**
- Stop all instances (no compute charges)
- Keep storage (minimal charges, e.g., $0.023/GB/month)
- Retain your data and configuration
- Resume anytime (no reactivation fee)

**Best For:**
- Seasonal businesses
- Projects on hold
- Cost reduction without data loss

**How to Pause:**
1. Stop all instances via console or CLI
2. Downgrade to Free tier (if eligible)
3. Keep only essential storage

## Account Termination (By Team Defaulters)

### Reasons for Termination

Team Defaulters reserves the right to terminate your account for:

1. **Non-Payment:**
   - Payment overdue by 30+ days
   - Credit card declined 3+ times

2. **Acceptable Use Policy Violations:**
   - Cryptocurrency mining (without prior approval)
   - DDoS attacks or network abuse
   - Hosting illegal content (piracy, malware, etc.)
   - Spamming or phishing

3. **Fraud:**
   - Using stolen credit cards
   - Creating multiple accounts to abuse trial credits
   - Reselling services without authorization

4. **Security Threats:**
   - Account compromised and used for malicious activity
   - Failure to remediate security vulnerabilities after notification

### Termination Process

**Step 1: Warning**
- Email notification of policy violation
- 7 days to remediate issue

**Step 2: Suspension**
- If issue not resolved, account is suspended
- Services stopped (data retained for 30 days)
- 15 days to resolve issue and pay any outstanding balances

**Step 3: Termination**
- If still unresolved, account is permanently terminated
- All data deleted within 30 days
- **No refunds issued**

**Immediate Termination (No Warning):**
- Severe violations (DDoS attacks, illegal content) result in immediate termination
- Law enforcement may be contacted

### Appealing a Termination

If you believe your account was terminated in error:

1. Email: appeals@teamdefaulters.com
2. Include:
   - Account ID
   - Explanation of circumstances
   - Evidence supporting your appeal
3. Response within 5 business days

**Note:** Appeals are rarely successful for clear policy violations.

## Data Retention After Cancellation

### Soft Delete Period (30 Days)

**What Happens:**
- Data is marked for deletion but not yet destroyed
- You can reactivate your account and recover data
- Storage charges continue to accrue (unless you manually delete data)

**How to Recover:**
1. Log in to console: console.teamdefaulters.com
2. Click "Reactivate Account"
3. Provide payment method
4. All data is restored

### Hard Delete Period (After 30 Days)

**What Happens:**
- Data is **permanently deleted** (cryptographic erasure)
- Encryption keys are destroyed (data is unrecoverable)
- Backups and snapshots are deleted

**No Recovery Possible:**
- Team Defaulters cannot recover data after hard delete
- This is irreversible

### Compliance Data Retention

**Audit Logs:**
- Retained for **1 year** after account closure (compliance requirement)
- Used only for legal/regulatory purposes
- Not accessible to you after account closure

**Billing Records:**
- Retained for **7 years** (IRS requirement)
- Includes invoices, payment history, tax records

## Special Circumstances

### Service Outages

**If Team Defaulters Fails to Meet SLA:**
- You may be eligible for service credits (see Service Level Agreement document)
- Service credits are NOT refunds (they apply to future invoices)
- Maximum credit: 100% of monthly fee for affected service

**No Refunds for Outages:**
- Outages do not qualify for cash refunds
- Service credits are the sole remedy

### Force Majeure

**Events Beyond Our Control:**
- Natural disasters (earthquakes, floods, hurricanes)
- War, terrorism, civil unrest
- Government actions (sanctions, shutdowns)
- Pandemics

**Policy:**
- Team Defaulters is not liable for service disruptions due to force majeure
- No refunds or credits issued for force majeure events
- We will make commercially reasonable efforts to restore service

### Billing Disputes

**Step 3: Resolution**

**Chargebacks:**

- We will work with you to resolve disputes
